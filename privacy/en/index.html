Yanpian (岩片) Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · 中文

In short: Yanpian collects no data from you. All processing happens locally on your iPhone. The app has no network functionality, no accounts, and no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Data processed on-device

To provide heart-rate overlay, subject follow, and live viewfinder, Yanpian accesses the following data only after your explicit authorization, and only on your device — it never leaves it:

  • Photo library (videos): reads the climbing videos you explicitly select for overlay and editing; exported results are saved back to your photo library. Video files are never uploaded.
  • Health (heart rate): read-only access via HealthKit to heart-rate samples recorded by your Apple Watch, used to overlay heart rate onto the matching moments of your video. Yanpian never writes, modifies, or uploads your health data.
  • Camera: used during live viewfinder to capture with the rear camera and detect people in the frame on-device. Recordings are saved to Photos and never uploaded.
  • Microphone: records ambient sound with live-viewfinder video, saved to Photos with the clip.
  • Bluetooth: connects your gimbal during live viewfinder so the camera can nudge when the person is about to leave the frame. Communication stays on-device with the gimbal; it does not go over the internet.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers;
  • No cookies, analytics, crash reporting, or ad tracking;
  • No account registration;
  • The app performs no network requests at all.

Children's privacy

Yanpian does not collect data from children — or from anyone.
