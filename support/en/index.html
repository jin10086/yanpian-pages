Yanpian (岩片) Support

中文

Yanpian overlays Apple Watch heart rate onto climbing videos and adds viewport keyframes for far-camera footage. It is an iPhone app.

Contact

For problems, feature ideas, or business, email igaojin@qq.com.

FAQ

Where does heart-rate data come from?
Yanpian reads heart-rate samples your Apple Watch already wrote to Apple Health. Confirm Health has samples for that time, and that Yanpian may read Heart Rate.

Are videos or heart rate uploaded?
No. Yanpian has no network features. Everything stays on device. See the privacy policy.

Which devices?
iPhone (iOS 17 or later). Heart-rate overlay needs samples already in Health (usually from Apple Watch). Live-viewfinder lock confirm can use the Watch app; recording works without a Watch.

What does the Watch app do?
Only opening lock during live viewfinder: after lock, the Watch shows the crop. Tap Not me if it isn’t you. It is not a heart-rate display and does not control the gimbal. Not installing or not opening it does not block recording.
